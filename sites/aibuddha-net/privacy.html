Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At AI Buddha, your peace of mind includes how your data is handled. We collect minimal data and never sell or share it.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Chat Conversations

Your conversations with AI Buddha are processed in real-time to generate responses. Messages are anonymized and not permanently stored. We do not retain chat history on our servers.

1.2 Meditation & Timer Data

The meditation timer runs entirely in your browser. Duration preferences are stored locally in localStorage and never sent to our servers. No personal health data is collected.

1.3 Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data: pages visited, features used, time spent. This helps us improve the experience. No personal identifiers are included.

1.4 Language Preferences

Your selected language (EN, NL, ES, DE, FR, HI) is saved in your browser's localStorage and never transmitted to our servers.

1.5 Cookies

We use zero tracking or advertising cookies. Only essential cookies for language preference and session functionality.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Generate AI Buddha responses to your questions
  • Improve the AI's mindfulness guidance and accuracy
  • Analyze anonymous trends to enhance the website
  • Comply with legal obligations when required

We do not: Sell your data, share it with advertisers, use tracking cookies, or build user profiles for commercial purposes.

3. Data Storage & Security

Chat processing occurs in-memory and data is not persistently stored. Language preferences remain in your browser. We implement industry-standard security measures for any transient data processing.

4. Third-Party Services

We embed YouTube videos for lofi music streaming. YouTube's privacy policy applies when you interact with embedded content. We use no other third-party trackers or analytics scripts.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Access — request any personal data we may hold
  • Correction — fix inaccurate information
  • Deletion — "right to be forgotten"
  • Portability — receive your data in a standard format
  • Objection — object to processing of your data

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page. Material changes may be communicated via a notice on the website.
