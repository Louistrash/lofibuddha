Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

At AI Buddha, we believe that peace of mind includes how your data is handled. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

AI Buddha collects minimal data:

  • Chat messages: Your conversations with AI Buddha are processed to generate responses. Messages are anonymized and not permanently stored.
  • Usage data: Anonymous page visits, features used, meditation timer usage.
  • Language preference: Stored locally in your browser.
🔒 We do NOT: Collect personal identifiers, sell data, use tracking cookies, or share anything with advertisers.

2. How We Use Information

  • Generate AI Buddha responses to your questions
  • Improve the AI's accuracy and mindfulness guidance
  • Anonymous analytics to improve the website

3. Data Storage

Chat conversations are processed in-memory and not persistently stored. Language preferences are saved in your browser's localStorage and never sent to our servers.

4. Third-Party Services

We use YouTube embeds for lofi music streaming. YouTube's privacy policy applies when you interact with embedded videos.

5. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, delete, or export any personal data we may hold. As we store minimal data, there is typically nothing to access.
