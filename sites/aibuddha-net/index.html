The Importance of Breathing

In Buddhism, breathing (Anapanasati) is the anchor to the present moment. By consciously directing your breath, you slow down the nervous system and calm the busy stream of thoughts ('monkey mind').

  • 4-4-4 Box breathing rhythm Our guide gently walks you through: inhale for 4 seconds, hold for 4 seconds, and exhale for 4 seconds.
  • Visual Anchor The breathing circle pulses in sync with the rhythm, so you can focus your eyes on it.
