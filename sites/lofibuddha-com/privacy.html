Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

🧘 At LofiBuddha, your privacy is fundamental to our philosophy. Peace of mind extends to how we handle your data.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create an account or subscribe, we collect your email address and name (optional). Payment information is processed securely through Stripe — we never see or store your credit card details.

1.2 Newsletter Subscriptions

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we store your email address to send you weekly calm tips and lofi mixes. You can unsubscribe at any time with one click.

1.3 Usage Analytics

We collect anonymous usage data: pages visited, content streamed, features used. This helps us improve the experience. No personal identifiers are included.

1.4 Language Preferences

Your language choice (EN, NL, ES, DE, FR, HI) is saved in your browser and never transmitted to our servers.

1.5 Cookies

We use zero advertising cookies. Only essential cookies for language, session, and — if you consent — anonymous analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • Deliver lofi music, yoga flows, and meditation content
  • Send newsletters and updates (only if opted in)
  • Process subscription payments via Stripe
  • Improve our content based on anonymous usage patterns

🚫 We do NOT: Sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it beyond delivering mindfulness content.

3. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers within the European Union. We use encryption, access controls, and regular security audits.

4. Third-Party Services

We use Stripe for payments, YouTube for embedded video content, and privacy-focused analytics. Each service has its own privacy policy.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)

  • Access — request any data we hold about you
  • Correction — fix inaccurate information
  • Deletion — right to be forgotten
  • Portability — export your data
  • Objection — object to data processing

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Changes to This Policy

Updates will be posted on this page. Material changes will be communicated to subscribers.
