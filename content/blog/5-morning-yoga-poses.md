## 5 Morning Yoga Poses for Beginners

You don't need to be flexible. You don't need a fancy mat. You just need 10 minutes and the willingness to meet your body where it is.

### 1. Child's Pose (Balasana) — 2 minutes

Kneel on the floor, big toes touching, knees apart. Fold forward, resting your forehead on the ground. Arms can reach forward or rest alongside your body.

*This pose signals your nervous system: "We're safe. We can relax."*

### 2. Cat-Cow (Marjaryasana-Bitilasana) — 2 minutes

On hands and knees, alternate between arching your spine (cow) and rounding it (cat). Move with your breath — inhale for cow, exhale for cat.

*Wakes up your spine after a night of stillness.*

### 3. Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana) — 1 minute

From hands and knees, tuck your toes and lift your hips toward the ceiling. Pedal your feet, bend your knees generously. This isn't about straight legs — it's about lengthening your spine.

*Gets blood flowing to your brain. Instant energy boost.*

### 4. Standing Forward Fold (Uttanasana) — 1 minute

Stand with feet hip-width apart, soft knees, fold forward. Let your head hang heavy. Grab opposite elbows and sway gently.

*Releases tension in your neck and lower back where stress accumulates overnight.*

### 5. Mountain Pose (Tadasana) — 2 minutes

Stand tall, feet grounded, arms at your sides. Close your eyes. Feel your weight evenly distributed. Take 10 slow breaths.

*You're not just standing — you're arriving. This is the most underrated pose in yoga.*

### Your Morning Ritual

Set a timer for 10 minutes. Move through these 5 poses at your own pace. Don't judge. Just breathe.
