# Beginner's Mindfulness — Complete Guide

## A 7-module journey into presence

---

### Module 1: The Breath as Anchor

Your breath is always with you. It is the simplest, most reliable anchor for attention.

**Practice:**
- Sit comfortably. Close your eyes or soften your gaze.
- Bring attention to the sensation of breathing — cool air entering your nostrils, the gentle rise of your chest.
- Don't try to change anything. Just notice.
- When your mind wanders (and it will — that's normal), gently return to the breath.

This is the entire practice: not staying focused, but returning. Each return is a bicep curl for your attention.

**Duration:** Start with 2 minutes. Add 1 minute each day until you reach 10.

---

### Module 2: Body Scan

The body is always in the present moment, even when the mind is elsewhere.

**Practice:**
- Lie down or sit comfortably. Close your eyes.
- Start at the crown of your head. Slowly move attention down: forehead, jaw, neck, shoulders, arms, hands.
- Continue: chest, belly, hips, legs, feet, toes.
- Notice any tension, warmth, tingling, numbness. Don't judge — just observe.
- If you notice tension, breathe into that area. Don't try to release it — just acknowledge it.

**Duration:** 10-15 minutes.

---

### Module 3: Labeling Thoughts

Thoughts are not the enemy. They're just mental events, like clouds passing through a vast sky.

**Practice:**
- Sit quietly. When a thought arises, silently label it.
- "Planning." "Worrying." "Remembering." "Judging."
- Then let it go — like a leaf floating downstream.
- Return to the breath.

This creates distance between *you* and your thoughts. You are the sky, not the clouds.

---

### Module 4: Walking Meditation

Meditation doesn't require sitting still.

**Practice:**
- Walk slowly, deliberately.
- Feel each foot touch the ground — heel, ball, toes.
- Match your breath to your steps: 2 steps in, 3 steps out.
- Notice the air on your skin, sounds around you, the rhythm of movement.
- When your mind wanders, return to the sensation of your feet.

The world becomes your meditation cushion.

---

### Module 5: Mindful Eating

One meal a day, eaten with full attention.

**Practice:**
- Choose one meal. Put away all screens.
- Before eating, pause. Look at your food. Notice colors, textures, arrangement.
- Take the first bite. Put your fork down. Chew slowly.
- Notice flavors, temperatures, the impulse to swallow.
- Between bites, breathe.

This transforms a daily habit into a meditation practice.

---

### Module 6: Loving-Kindness (Metta)

Compassion is a skill — it can be cultivated.

**Practice:**
- Sit quietly. Picture yourself. Silently repeat:
  - *May I be happy. May I be healthy. May I be safe. May I live with ease.*
- Now picture someone you love. Same phrases.
- A neutral person (someone you see but don't know well).
- Someone you find difficult.
- Finally: all beings everywhere.

Notice resistance. That's information — not failure.

---

### Module 7: Open Awareness

The culmination: dropping all focus, all effort.

**Practice:**
- Sit quietly. Don't focus on anything.
- Don't push anything away.
- Let sounds, sensations, thoughts come and go — like clouds across a vast sky.
- You are the sky. Not the clouds.

If you find yourself focusing, gently release. Rest in awareness itself.

**Duration:** 10-20 minutes.

---

### Continuing Your Practice

- The goal is not to empty your mind. It's to change your relationship with what's in it.
- Some days will feel easy. Some will feel impossible. Both are practice.
- The only "bad" meditation is the one you didn't do.
